Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

In short: Moonlight Read doesn't collect, sell, or share any information about you or your child. Stories, books, and settings stay on your own device.

What stays on your device

  • Your child's first name and age, if you enter them in Parents, used only for greetings and Story Maker stories.
  • Stories you make, books you add, reading settings, and bedtime stats.

Services a grown-up connects

Moonlight Read works without any outside service. If a grown-up connects their own ElevenLabs, OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), or Google Gemini account in Parents, Moonlight Read asks first, then sends only what that feature needs, directly from the device to the chosen service:

  • Lifelike voices: the story text being read aloud.
  • Story Maker: the choices for the story, including the child's first name and age if entered.

The key is stored only in the device's Keychain and is sent only to that service. We never receive it or anything sent with it. That service's own terms and privacy policy apply. Remove a key at any time in Parents to stop.

Purchases

Moonlight Read Plus is an in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple, behind the grown-up check. We never see your name, card, or Apple Account.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • No analytics, tracking, or accounts.
  • No data sold or shared with anyone.

Children

Moonlight Read is made for families. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
