Moonlight Read Support

Moonlight Read is a bedtime read-along for kids. A calm storyteller reads aloud, every word lights up as it's said, and Lights Out ends the night with a gentle goodnight.

Need help? Email manbem13@myyahoo.com. Tell us which device you're using (iPhone or iPad) and what happened, and include a screenshot if you can.

Stories

  • Moonlight Originals: 19 bedtime stories written for the app, including Hugo the Hedgehog, plus gentle retellings of classic fables.
  • The Classics Library: beloved children's books old enough to belong to everyone, ready to add from Parents.
  • Story Maker: a brand-new story starring your child. Everyone can make 3 stories to try.

The grown-up check

Settings, Story Maker, voices, adding books, and purchases are all in Parents, behind a quick question only a grown-up can answer.

Moonlight Read Plus

Moonlight Read Plus is a one-time in-app purchase, yours to keep, with no subscription. It adds:

  • Unlimited Story Maker stories.
  • Lifelike storytellers from your own ElevenLabs or Google Gemini account.
  • Your own books: PDF, EPUB, and text files you own, read aloud word by word.

Already bought it? Open Parents and tap Restore Purchases.

Voices and story writing

  • The standard voice built into your device always works, with no account.
  • Story Maker writes with Apple Intelligence on supported devices, or with your own OpenAI, Claude, or Google Gemini account.
  • Keys you add stay in your device's Keychain and go only to the service you chose. That service bills you directly.

Privacy

Moonlight Read has no ads, no tracking, and no accounts. Read the Privacy Policy.
